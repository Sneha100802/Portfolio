# Sneha Jha — Antique Indian Illustrated Journal & Portfolio
> **"An Elegant Indian Illustrated Journal Transformed into an AI Engineer's Portfolio"**

A portfolio website designed as an **open antique Indian illustrated journal** with two visible pages, a warm ivory handmade paper cover with delicate Madhubani-inspired floral motifs, soft antique gold details, dusty rose and sage accents, thick textured parchment paper, a central spine crease, and detailed watercolor paintings for every major project and journey milestone.

---

## 📖 Key Refinements & User Experience

1. **New Warm Ivory Illustrated Journal Cover**:
   - Replaced the heavy dark burgundy leather cover with an **elegant Indian illustrated journal on warm ivory handmade paper**.
   - Accented with subtle Madhubani-inspired double-line floral fillets in dusty rose (`#c87683`) and muted sage (`#5f7f64`).
   - Features a delicate, arched watercolor vignette of New Delhi's India Gate with soft gold trim.
   - Elegant typography: *Sneha Jha* in deep maroon-plum ink (`#481422`), with role subtitle and *"A Journey Through Code, Culture, and Creation"* tagline.
   - Refined **"Open Portfolio · Explore Index"** pill button with subtle warm illumination.

2. **Ambient Watercolor Background**:
   - Replaced the dark background with an ambient blend of **warm parchment beige, soft ivory, and subtle watercolor washes** (dusty rose at top-left, muted sage at bottom-right), creating visual harmony with the interior pages.
   - Default theme is Light Mode, with a dark mode toggle that switches to a deep, warm indigo-plum ink paper aesthetic.

3. **Interactive Table of Contents / Index (Chapter 00)**:
   - Opening the book now transitions directly to a dedicated two-page **Table of Contents**:
     * Headline: *"What would you like to discover about me?"*
     * Subtitle: *"Select a chapter below to explore my engineering journey, AI research, and projects."*
   - Features all 8 interactive chapter cards (4 on Left Page, 4 on Right Page):
     1. **Chapter 01: About Me** (`p. 01`) — *Profile, core engineering pillars & philosophy* (🍃)
     2. **Chapter 02: My Journey** (`p. 03`) — *New Delhi schooling, Bihar roots & systems growth* (📍)
     3. **Chapter 03: Education & Research** (`p. 05`) — *CSVTU Bhilai Honours & IIIT Naya Raipur research* (🎓)
     4. **Chapter 04: Experience** (`p. 07`) — *Survatra sole backend developer & Blazetrail fintech* (💼)
     5. **Chapter 05: Technical Projects** (`p. 07`) — *Multimodal RAG, Hotel Q&A, Multi-Agent & Audio ML* (💻)
     6. **Chapter 06: Skills & Toolchains** (`p. 09`) — *Languages, AI/LLMs, Vector DBs & Cloud* (⚙️)
     7. **Chapter 07: Publications** (`p. 11`) — *IEEE ICCCNT & IEEE TENSYMP 2024 peer-reviewed papers* (📖)
     8. **Chapter 08: Contact & Résumé** (`p. 13`) — *Direct email, LinkedIn, GitHub & downloadable résumé* (✉️)

4. **Integrated In-Page Navigation (No Floating External Tabs)**:
